What is orthodontics and why might I need it?

Orthodontics corrects the position of the teeth and jaws. It improves appearance, but also chewing, speech and long-term oral health.

At what age should orthodontic treatment start?

A first check is recommended around age 7. Treatment can then start at the right moment, and adults can be treated at any age.

What types of braces are available?

External braces (metal or ceramic), internal braces placed behind the teeth, clear aligners and removable appliances for children.

What are clear aligners?

A series of transparent, removable trays that move the teeth step by step according to a digital plan.

How long do I need to wear aligners each day?

Usually around 22 hours a day, removing them only to eat and brush your teeth.

How long does orthodontic treatment last?

Most treatments last between 12 and 24 months, depending on the complexity of the case.

Is orthodontic treatment painful?

You may feel some pressure for a few days after each adjustment. It is usually mild and short-lived.

How often will I see the orthodontist?

Generally every 6 to 10 weeks, with some follow-ups possible remotely.

Do I need special care during treatment?

Careful brushing, regular check-ups and a few food precautions with fixed braces.
