Our concept

Our values

We believe a smile is much more than aligned teeth. It expresses personality, a delicate balance between harmony and emotion. Each smile is unique and should blend with the rest of the face.

Our mission is to reveal the potential of every smile so it reflects each person’s natural beauty. Beyond appearance, we make sure every smile is balanced and comfortable, taking into account the jaw joints, chewing, speech and breathing.

Our priority is a result that brings aesthetics and health together, for a smile you wear with confidence every day.

Smile and facial
structural diagnosis

Your smile deserves a global vision: attention to every line and every detail of your face. Our structural diagnosis is the moment where we read the geometry of your smile. Using modern imaging, we study how the smile and the face interact to plan a harmonious result.

Analysis and
treatment plan

In the clinic or remotely, we design a treatment plan that follows your wishes and respects your needs. It is personalised in every detail and you are guided by our experts at each step, with close follow-up until the result matches your expectations.

Make an appointment

To make things easier, we also offer remote consultations. If you would like an appointment quickly, a first short visit can be used to prepare the administrative documents and create your patient file.

After that, most of the diagnosis and follow-up can take place remotely, from home. Secure, personalised tools keep the quality of care identical to an in-clinic visit.
