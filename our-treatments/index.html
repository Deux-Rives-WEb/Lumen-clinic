Lumen expertise

Our expertise

Every smile is unique, and so is every treatment plan. We combine orthodontics, aesthetic care and prevention so that the result looks natural, works well and lasts. Each plan starts with a careful diagnosis and is adjusted at every visit.

Our commitments

01. Innovation

Clinical excellence goes hand in hand with comfort. The practice is equipped with current technology so treatments are effective and pleasant.

02. Patient relationship

We take a patient-centred approach with clear, open communication at every stage, so you always know what comes next.

03. Research

To keep pace with medical progress, the team takes part in research, conferences and training throughout the year.

Our treatments

OrthodonticsAestheticsRegeneration

Fixed or removable

We offer a full range of orthodontic treatments for every need.

Fixed treatments

A proven classic: whether on the outer or inner surface of the teeth, fixed appliances provide reliable, lasting correction. Available in metal or ceramic, with options tailored for comfort.

Removable treatments

Clear aligners combine the effectiveness of conventional braces with the flexibility and hygiene of a removable appliance. For children, early treatment guides jaw growth and aligns teeth discreetly.
